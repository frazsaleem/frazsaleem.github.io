Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy covers our mobile games Dot Defense TD and Tile Dash 20 (the "apps"). We have tried to keep it short and in plain English.

Who we are

FSJ Studio is the developer of the apps. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean FSJ Studio.

What the apps store on your device

The apps store your game progress on your device. There are no accounts, and we do not ask for your name or any other personal details.

Third-party services

The apps use a few third-party services. They may collect data as described below, under their own privacy policies.

Google Firebase Analytics

Collects usage events (such as which levels are played), device and app information, an app-instance ID, and approximate location derived from your IP address.

AppLovin (advertising)

Used to show and measure ads. It may collect your advertising ID, device information, IP address and ad interactions. See the AppLovin privacy policy.

Google AdMob (possible future use)

We may use Google AdMob for ads in the future. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Google Play

Google Play processes data when you download, update or use apps from the store, under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Why we use this data

  • Analytics, so we can understand how the games are used and improve them.
  • Advertising, which helps keep the games free. This includes optional rewarded videos, which you can choose to watch in return for an in-game reward.

Consent and choices

Where required, including in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, the apps show a consent prompt before ads or analytics data are used. You can change your choice where the apps offer that option.

On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads. Open Settings and look for Privacy (or Google), then Ads. The exact menu names vary by device. Resetting the ID or opting out does not remove ads, but they will be less personalised.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

In-app purchases

There are no in-app purchases at present. If we add any, they will be processed by Google Play. FSJ Studio never receives your payment details.

Retention

Firebase Analytics keeps event data for its default period (for example, 14 months). Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. Data held by other services follows their own retention policies.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the data involved, and we keep the amount we collect small. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be reflected in the apps or on our Google Play pages where appropriate.

Contact
