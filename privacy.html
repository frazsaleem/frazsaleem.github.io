Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy covers our mobile games Dot Defense TD and Tile Dash 20 (the "apps"). We have tried to keep it short and in plain English.

Who we are

FSJ Studio is the developer of the apps. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean FSJ Studio.

What the apps store on your device

The apps store your game progress on your device. There are no accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details. On Android, Auto Backup may copy your game progress to your own Google account, under Google's terms. We do not see that backup.

Third-party services and the data they collect

The apps use a few third-party services. They collect data as described below and handle it under their own privacy policies.

Unity LevelPlay (ironSource Ads), for advertising

We show ads through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service from Unity (which includes ironSource). LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with may collect your Android advertising ID, approximate location (derived from your IP address), your IP address, device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, language and network type), and how you interact with ads. This is used to show ads, measure them, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing, for in-app purchases

The apps offer optional in-app purchases (such as Dots, Tower Points, card packs, premium towers and the Starter Pack). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your card or other payment details. We receive a purchase token and order status from Google so that we can check the purchase and give you the item. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Firebase Analytics and Remote Config

Firebase Analytics collects usage events (such as which levels are played), device and app information, an app-instance ID and approximate location derived from your IP address. We use it to understand how the games are used and to fix problems. Analytics works with Google's consent mode, so where you have declined consent it is limited accordingly. Firebase Remote Config lets us change game settings (for example balance values) without releasing an update, and uses an app-instance ID to deliver them. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play services

Google Play and Google Play services process data when you download, update or use apps from the store, and provide the advertising ID, under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Why we use this data

  • Advertising, which helps keep the games free. This includes optional rewarded videos, which you can choose to watch in return for an in-game reward.
  • In-app purchases, so we can confirm a purchase and deliver what you bought.
  • Analytics and app configuration, so we can understand how the games are used and improve them.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data and we do not collect names or contact details. Data is shared only with the services listed above (Unity LevelPlay and its ad network partners, Google and Firebase), which receive it directly from the app. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Consent and how to withdraw it

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the apps show a consent prompt the first time they are used, with Accept and Decline choices, before advertising or analytics data is used. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices.

On Android you can also reset your advertising ID, or delete it or opt out of ad personalisation. Open your phone's Settings and look for Privacy (or Google), then Ads. The exact menu names vary by device. Resetting the ID or opting out does not remove ads, but they will be less personalised.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, and are rated 13+ on Google Play. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and our ads are not child-directed. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will help get it removed from the services involved.

Data retention

We keep no user accounts or profiles. Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. Firebase Analytics keeps event data for its configured period (up to 14 months). Data held by Unity, Google and ad networks follows their own retention policies, linked above.

Your rights and deletion requests

Because we do not collect your name or contact details, we usually cannot identify data about you ourselves. You can still contact us at the address below to ask about, or ask for deletion of, data linked to you, and we will do what we reasonably can, including pointing you to the relevant provider. Uninstalling the app and resetting your advertising ID breaks the link between you and previously collected data.

If you are in the EEA or the United Kingdom, you have rights under data protection law, including to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Our legal basis for advertising and analytics data is your consent where it is required, and otherwise our legitimate interest in running and improving the games. For purchases it is performance of the purchase contract.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the data involved, and we keep the amount we collect small. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be reflected in the apps or on our Google Play pages where appropriate.
